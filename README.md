
# 🚀 CSK AI/ML MINI-HACKATHON

### *Think. Build. Innovate.*

Welcome to the **Computer Society of Kirinyaga (CSK) AI/ML Mini-Hackathon!**

It's time to move beyond theory and put your AI/ML knowledge into practice. This is your opportunity to experiment, collaborate, solve problems, and build something meaningful.

Whether you're just getting started with AI/ML or already have some experience, you're welcome to participate!

---

## 🎯 1. Hackathon Theme

**AI for Everyday Campus Life**

## 💡 2. The Challenge

Students often struggle to keep track of university announcements, events, deadlines, and other important information.

Your task is to **build an AI-powered solution that helps students access, understand, or organize important campus information more effectively.**

You are free to decide how to approach the problem.

---

## 👥 3. Participation Guidelines

- You can participate **individually or in a team of up to 3 members**.
- Participants of all AI/ML experience levels are welcome.
- Each individual or team must develop and submit their own solution.
- Collaboration within registered teams is encouraged.
- Your solution should demonstrate a meaningful application of AI/ML.

---

## 🛠️ 4. Technical Guidelines

Python is recommended as the primary programming language.

You may use tools and libraries such as:

- Python
- NumPy and Pandas
- Scikit-learn
- TensorFlow / Keras
- PyTorch
- Streamlit
- Google Colab

You are not restricted to the tools listed above. Choose technologies that best suit your approach.

**Important:** We are looking for a functional solution, not just an idea or presentation.

---

## 📦 5. What to Submit

Each participant or team is expected to provide:

1. **Working Prototype:** A functional demonstration of your solution.
2. **Source Code:** A GitHub repository containing your implementation.
3. **Documentation:** A README explaining your solution, technologies used, and setup instructions.
4. **Demonstration:** A short video or demonstration showcasing how your solution works.

Ensure your GitHub repository is public.

## 🏆 7. Evaluation Criteria

Submissions will be evaluated out of **100 marks** using the following criteria:

| Criteria | Marks |
|:---|:---:|
| Problem Understanding & Relevance | 15 |
| AI/ML Approach & Implementation | 25 |
| Functionality & Usefulness | 25 |
| Creativity & Innovation | 15 |
| Presentation & Demonstration | 10 |
| Code Quality, Documentation & Teamwork | 10 |
| **TOTAL** | **100** |

### How Winners Will Be Selected

- A panel of 2–3 judges will evaluate the submissions.
- Judges will use the same evaluation criteria for all participants.
- Final scores will be calculated by averaging the judges' scores.
- In case of a tie, the participant or team with the higher combined score in AI/ML implementation and functionality will rank higher.

## 🌟 9. A Few Things to Remember

- Understand the problem before jumping into implementation.
- Focus on building something useful and functional.
- Test your solution before submission.
- Document your work properly.
- Don't be afraid to experiment and try different approaches.

Remember, a simple, well-executed solution is better than an ambitious idea that doesn't work.
